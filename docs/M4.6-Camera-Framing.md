# M4.6 camera framing correction

The full-departure fit below is now the **1.00x** option. Current production zoom
and the deliberate exit-clipping policy are described in [Adjustable Zoom](M4.6-Adjustable-Zoom.md).

The accepted native baseline was **167/167 EditMode tests passed**. This change
only adjusts the runtime framing profile for boards taller than 10 cells (the
existing 8x11 Levels 7 and 8). Open the existing FeedbackCampaign and press Play
after compilation; no asset or scene generation is required.

## Limitation and correction

The previous fitter combined the board, all possible fully departed blocks and
burst envelopes into one world-space AABB, then projected its eight corners.
It reserved 32 reference pixels on either side and 72 below even for outgoing
effects, and kept a minimum orthographic size inherited from the small boards.
The burst envelope also combined maximum travel, arc and unshrunk fragment radius
as if they could occur simultaneously. Projecting the combined world AABB added
nonexistent combinations of height and longitudinal extremes.

The **dominant horizontal limit is real geometry**, rather than the burst:
the green two-cell-wide L exits west and the purple two-cell-wide block exits
east. Each whole block remains visible at full size until the 0.18-second outward
phase completes. The union of their departure bounds, including the existing
contact shadows, is **12 x 0.68 = 8.16 world units** wide. The accepted tray is
6 units wide, so even a margin-free fixed camera could show it at only **73.53%**
of viewport width; the 8-cell grid span could reach at most **66.67%**.
The approved tray has world dimensions 6x8, distinct from the logical 8x11 grid.
An 85–90% tray width is incompatible with retaining both complete departures in
one fixed view. No camera tracking or animation changes were introduced to force
that percentage.

The maximum-height profile now:

- Projects separate conservative geometry bounds instead of the combined world
  AABB, including the actual authored tray renderers using the theme's tray material.
- Includes every matching gate/footprint, every possible alignment and all whole
  block departure positions, including selection lift and contact shadows.
- Bounds fragments over 32 continuous time intervals. Eased travel is monotonic;
  sine extrema and the largest shrinking radius in each interval enclose every
  instant and rotation. This is not a fit to sparse sampled animation poses.
- Reserves **8 reference pixels** at each safe-area side and **24 below** for
  departures. The full **316-reference-pixel HUD exclusion** remains: the 280-pixel
  top panel, 12-pixel offset and 24-pixel separation. Insets use the accepted
  CanvasScaler's geometric-mean scale. A further **0.2%** numeric fit margin remains.
- Fits the largest safe static composition without the inherited minimum-size
  floor. It translates only in the camera image plane and changes orthographic
  size. Depth, pitch, rotation, grid/root scale and pointer mapping are unchanged.
- Caches the geometry projection when a level is constructed; resolution and safe
  area changes only recalculate the scalar fit. Active effects never change the
  fit, so repeated drags, glows and bursts cannot cause camera breathing or jitter.

Levels 1–6 retain the exact previous profile and calculations. No serialized
camera settings, scene YAML, level/solver/witness data, collider, gate aperture,
theme/lighting/material, audio, input or animation code changed.

## Geometric measurements

Both existing 8x11 levels have the same horizontal limit. These measurements use
the accepted camera direction and a full-screen safe area. Percentages refer to
the **full usable viewport width**, not to a selected crop of it.

| Resolution | Previous tray width | Corrected tray width | 8-cell grid span | Ortho size | Cell pitch X / projected Z |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1080x1920 | 68.345% | 72.295% | 65.548% | 7.377131 | 88.490 / 79.148 px |
| 1080x2400 | 67.837% | 72.167% | 65.432% | 9.237810 | 88.333 / 79.007 px |
| 720x1280 | 68.345% | 72.295% | 65.548% | 7.377131 | 58.993 / 52.765 px |

The side-exit constraint consumes all available horizontal space, within the
explicit gutters and numeric margin. Additional vertical space is unavoidable
at this camera pitch/aspect while keeping a fixed camera and unchanged exits.
The union is centered in the safe region below the HUD. The complete tray,
board and gates also remain within the older, stricter 32/72/316-pixel gameplay
region at all three tested resolutions, including asymmetric safe-area insets
(40 left, 24 right, 64 bottom, 96 top at 1080 reference width).
A separate height-constrained safe-area case verifies that complete visibility
takes priority when available height becomes the limiting dimension.
With the tested asymmetric insets, tray utilization is **72.227% of safe width**
at 1080x1920 / 720x1280 and **72.091%** at 1080x2400.
